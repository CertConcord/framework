# VeriCommons Trademark Policy 1.0

VeriCommons identifies an implementation and its related brand. CertConcord is the neutral specification name. Implementations may use their own names and may describe their supported CertConcord editions and profiles accurately.

## Rights and identification

Copyright licenses and specification patent commitments do not grant a general trademark license. Rights holders, marks, relevant jurisdictions or goods/services, authority evidence and appointed administrators are recorded in the [rights register](reference/governance/registry.json). A repository account or this policy does not establish trademark ownership or registration. No registered-trademark symbol may be used without a verified applicable registration.

Accurate, proportionate textual reference to VeriCommons for identification, attribution, interoperability or compatibility is permitted by this policy to the extent the recorded holder controls the relevant use; applicable rights of descriptive and nominative use remain unaffected. Such references must identify the user's own product and must not suggest sponsorship, origin, certification or endorsement that has not been granted.

## Permission for branding

Use as a product name, primary logo, domain identity or apparent official service requires an express grant from the recorded holder or its authorized administrator. A request identifies the applicant, exact mark/artwork, products, territories, media, term and proposed presentation. The holder's grant specifies its scope, approved artwork, quality conditions, review and termination terms. Only that executed grant can create permission for a reserved use.

Modified software and independent implementations must identify their own publisher and modifications. Attribution notices may be retained without presenting a modified implementation as an official VeriCommons release. The policy does not limit the freedoms granted by Apache-2.0, CC BY 4.0, CC0 or any executed specification patent instrument.

## Administration

The registrar verifies the holder and representative's authority before publishing a mark or grant as active. Complaints identify the disputed use and evidence; the administrator supplies a written decision and correction or appeal process to the affected party. Material misrepresentation, an expired grant or a valid suspension requires removal of the affected promotional use according to the grant. Historical factual references and legally protected uses remain governed by applicable law.

The [registration forms](reference/governance/forms.md) and register record the actual holder and grants. Without a recorded holder and executed grant, this policy makes no claim to supply that holder's permission. Conformance designations use the separate [Conformance mark policy](docs/conformance-marks.md).
