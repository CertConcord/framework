# Draft 03 acceptance scope

This working draft records a bounded document-verification contract. It does not make a stable or complete-framework conformance claim. The [requirement map](../spec/conformance.json) retains partial coverage and the [conformance record](../spec/conformance.md) separates executable draft evidence from independent implementation and operational assessment.

| Selected boundary                                                               | Executable evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Four result classes, stable identity and reasons, and only evaluated dimensions | `reference/sdk-conformance.test.mjs`; an applicable established failure takes precedence over unavailable or unsupported evidence                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Native mdoc original-byte signatures and shared component contract              | `reference/mdoc-document-boundary.test.mjs`; component/framework cross-consumption, exact critical-header order, signature mathematics and explicit knowledge time                                                                                                                                                  |
| Offline document, preservation and retained-decision replay                     | [Public corpus](../reference/vectors/document-acceptance-mtc07-rar2/README.md) and `node reference/verify-vectors.mjs`; the installed SDK consumes the document cases through its public export                                                                                                                                |

Original evidence and external trust inputs remain separate. An authentic receipt supplies operation time only after its complete operation tuple is bound to the current document. Native issuer inspection establishes signature and structural facts; complete credential verification still requires the bound operation time. Explicit knowledge time governs timestamped credential-log checkpoints and mirrors, including their future limits.

The native mdoc wire selection uses the pinned component's credential-first critical-header order. The earlier framework order is rejected. The public corpus retains synthetic original bytes for the selected current contract; component cross-consumption uses one shared implementation and is not evidence of a second independently developed verifier.

The live organization-delegation application remains **CURRENT_STATE_ONLY**. The separate retrospective API below grants no current execution permission. Full independent PAdES LTA validation is **not established**. The ISO 18013-5:2021 source record retains **`fullNormativeTextReviewed=false`**. These limits remain applicable after successful local and CI checks.

## Historical organization evidence

The dedicated [`createOrganizationHistoryVerifier`](organization-history.md) implements bounded offline verification of the existing single-path X509/CMS organization-delegation package, addressing CC-DOC-02 and CC-DOC-05. It verifies the original grant path, approver registrations and assertions, approval request, final authorization, permit, receipt, status and scoped authority history against an authenticated first complete-package ERS proof. The signed receipt supplies operation time A; the exact proof upper bound P includes fractional accuracy; constructor-bound current knowledge supplies K. The verifier requires `A <= P <= K`.

Historical authorization evaluates P under knowledge at P. Current admissibility evaluates the same historical operation under knowledge at K, while preservation checks continuous protection through K. All three must be `VALID`. Current successors cannot replace missing original evidence, and later GOOD status cannot erase admitted compromise or conflicts. Historical acceptance grants no current signing, reconciliation, recovery or output-release permission; the live application retains its current policy and journal checks.

The 55 cases in [`organization-history-independent.test.mjs`](../reference/organization-history-independent.test.mjs) use generated synthetic fixtures with real signatures, timestamp tokens and EvidenceRecords. They cover issuer expiry and scoped succession, missing retained status or original approval evidence, later-known compromise, altered approvals, fractional proof bounds and late renewal. The separate [organization corpus](../reference/vectors/organization-history-rar2/README.md) freezes 52 cases in 200 public material files: exact original bytes, external configuration, signed knowledge and complete expected results. Its independent semantic assertions precede result capture; replay requires no generation, signing, network or private state. The [installed-package consumer](../reference/sdk/consumer-test.mjs) replays the saved cases through the installed public source and verifier factories. These checks share the reference implementation and do not establish a second complete implementation.

The optional [SQLite knowledge source](organization-history-source.md#durable-source) retains raw authenticated authority/status material and exact original/ERS pairs under one immutable configuration. The durability suites exercise real process termination before append, before COMMIT and after COMMIT, competing writers, malformed and unbound siblings, immutable configuration, bounded capacity, partial corruption and recovery of exact bytes. The installed SDK also compares complete saved-byte results across distinct processes with clock, network, signing and generation disabled. Retained negative knowledge cannot supply fresh positive status or current execution permission.

The [separately preserved credential-status selection](organization-history.md#original-authorization-and-current-knowledge) accepts an optional RFC 4998 EvidenceRecord over the exact complete CertificateStatus CMS. It admits the status authority at publication S and the first status proof P_status under knowledge K, requires `S <= P_status <= K`, and verifies continuous protection through K. Timely preservation can retain authentication after that signer retires; it cannot replace original-package evidence or current positive-acceptance coverage, and authenticated negative knowledge remains applicable. The [saved preserved-status corpus](../reference/vectors/preserved-certificate-status-rar2/README.md) exercises this bounded selection, including proof-time distinctions and durable negative replay.

External OrganizationDelegationStatus may use the same complete-CMS preservation checks with exact typed-target and status-authority bindings. Its [saved public-byte corpus](../reference/vectors/preserved-delegation-status-rar2/README.md) covers the three admitted object types, exclusive deadlines, duplicate proof obligations and original-P authority retirement. Organization-status replay keeps the required original evidence pair and exact status ERS; fresh-process tests retain authenticated compromise and reject a changed ERS even when storage content hashes are recomputed. Current positive coverage and original-package proof obligations remain mandatory.

The selection retains the existing bounded grant path and selected CMS algorithms. Organizational mdoc, arbitrary delegation graphs, per-object proofs for original-package authorities retired before the first complete-package proof, detection of complete database/WAL rollback without an external anchor, and independent identity or custody assessment remain outside this evidence.
