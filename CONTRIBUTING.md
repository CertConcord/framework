# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome for implementation fixes, documentation, interoperability results, specification proposals and original research. Describe the problem, the proposed behavior and the evidence needed to review it. Draft proposals may include unresolved questions.

## Licenses and attribution

Original material intentionally submitted for inclusion is offered under the license applicable to that material in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md): Apache-2.0 for software and executable schemas, CC BY 4.0 for specifications and documentation, and CC0 1.0 for original synthetic interoperability vectors. No copyright assignment is required.

Submit material that you have the right to contribute. Identify incorporated third-party material, retain its notices and state any known license or rights restrictions. Do not include private credentials, real identity data or confidential material. Contributions subject to an employer's or another rights holder's authorization require that authorization.

A [DCO sign-off](reference/governance/DCO-1.1.txt) may be supplied by a contributor who chooses to make that certification. It is not a requirement for opening or merging a pull request. Existing authorship, attribution and sign-offs remain intact; automation metadata does not identify a legal rights holder.

## Technical review

Changes are reviewed for correctness, scope, security implications and compatibility with the shared certificate trust core. Run the relevant checks and describe any validation limits. Specification changes should identify affected requirements, selected mechanisms, security assumptions and positive and negative examples.

Ordinary code, documentation and working-draft specification contributions do not require a separate CLA, identity-document submission, registrar appointment or entry in a legal register. Merge decisions use technical review and the applicable license and provenance information.

## Specification rights

Copyright licensing and specification patent commitments are separate. Disclose known restrictions relevant to the submitted material; no patent search is required. A working draft does not imply universal patent coverage or a final specification patent commitment.

[PATENTS.md](PATENTS.md) describes the separate review of specification commitments before a stable edition is proposed. Any voluntary agreement requires its actual rights holder and an explicit scope. It does not become a condition for unrelated contributions.
