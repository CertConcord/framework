# Specification patent policy

CertConcord is a working draft. No executed specification patent commitment is recorded in the current [register](reference/governance/registry.json). Repository ownership, commit metadata and publication of this policy do not establish a patent grant.

## Contributions

Code, documentation and working-draft specification contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). They do not require a separate patent agreement or registration process. Contributors identify known rights restrictions relevant to their submissions; the project requires no patent search.

CC BY 4.0 and CC0 do not grant patent rights. Apache-2.0 provides the patent license defined by its own terms for contributions under that license. Those terms do not establish blanket patent coverage for every specification described or referenced by this repository.

## Specification commitments

The [OWF CLA 1.0, Copyright and Patent](https://www.openwebfoundation.org/the-agreements/the-owf-1-0-agreements-granted-claims/owf-contributor-license-agreement-1-0-copyright-and-patent) and [OWFa 1.0, Patent Only](https://www.openwebfoundation.org/the-agreements/the-owf-1-0-agreements-granted-claims/owfa-1-0-patent-only) are available mechanisms for explicit specification commitments. Their official terms remain unmodified. Selecting, linking or preparing an agreement does not execute it.

A voluntary commitment identifies the actual rights holder, authorized signatory, covered specification and scope. A final specification commitment additionally identifies the immutable edition, source revision, manifest digest and required portions. Later revisions are assessed separately; a record must not silently broaden an earlier commitment.

Existing executed agreements, notices and withdrawal rights continue to be governed by their own terms. For a contribution submitted under an executed OWF CLA, its 45-day withdrawal provision is preserved. That agreement-specific provision is not a waiting period for ordinary pull requests.

The optional [record tools](reference/governance/README.md) can retain reviewed instruments and their scopes. These records are separate from routine contribution review. Sensitive signature and identity material is excluded from public source history.

## Stable editions

Before proposing a stable specification, maintainers review the applicable patent policy, identified rights restrictions, required specification scope and actual commitments. The proposal reports unresolved coverage and any agreement-specific restrictions accurately. Passing tests or completing a register does not establish third-party patent clearance.

Conformance marks and assessments do not create patent rights. An external standard merely referenced by CertConcord does not receive a new patent license through that reference.
