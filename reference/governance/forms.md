# Registration schedules

These optional schedules support explicitly selected agreement or assessment processes. They are not required for ordinary code, documentation or working-draft specification contributions. They do not modify official agreements, constitute signatures or identify an actual rights holder by default. Leave no unresolved field in an executed instrument. A registrar assigns an ID only after the evidence has been reviewed.

## Holder and representative

| Field              | Required content                                                                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Holder             | Actual individual or legal entity name; jurisdiction and entity identifier where applicable |
| Bound entities     | Entities included under the official agreement's definitions                                |
| Representative     | Name, capacity, authority basis, authority-evidence digest and custodian                    |
| Contact            | Contact for notices; publish only the approved public contact                               |
| Execution evidence | Method, date, verified identity and preserved instrument reference                          |
| Registrar review   | Reviewer, verified authority, decision, UTC time and authenticated receipt                  |

## Final specification patent commitment

Attach the unmodified [OWFa 1.0 Patent-Only](https://www.openwebfoundation.org/the-agreements/the-owf-1-0-agreements-granted-claims/owfa-1-0-patent-only) and complete its individual or bound-entity signature fields. Its specification identification includes the CertConcord name, edition, exact revision, snapshot digest and identified conformance unit with all required portions. Record the full executed instrument digest, public receipt, custodian and any transfer/notice references. Do not record an unchecked template as an executed agreement.

## Normative contribution agreement and acknowledgement

Attach the unmodified [OWF CLA 1.0, Copyright and Patent](https://www.openwebfoundation.org/the-agreements/the-owf-1-0-agreements-granted-claims/owf-contributor-license-agreement-1-0-copyright-and-patent). Identify CertConcord and the specification-development scope, then complete the appropriate official signature fields and holder schedule. Record the contributor identities authorized under that holder's agreement.

The separate submission acknowledgement identifies the contribution and confirms authorization to distribute original normative prose under CC BY 4.0 and executable portions under Apache-2.0, with the official CLA's rights and withdrawal provision preserved. It records known third-party material and its terms. It is signed by the contributor or authorized representative and retained with the agreement record.

For each submission, record the exact content digest, original submission URI/channel, receipt time and attribution. A withdrawal notice identifies the exact material, the original channel, the notice time and preserved written notice. Review whether it is within the official 45-day period; flag disputed or unsettled material for exclusion from a final snapshot until resolved. Final OWFa execution is a separate act.

## Mark holder and designation

Record the exact mark text/artwork digest, claimed holder, jurisdiction and relevant goods/services, applicable registrations if any, evidence of authority to administer the mark, policy version/digest and designated registrar. An implementation repository or project name alone is insufficient ownership evidence.

## Conformance grant

Record the grantee, implementation/version/environment, source/build identity, configuration digest, specification snapshot and all selected profiles/adapters. Attach the approved requirement-to-evidence plan, authorized assessor record, report/artifact digests and reviewed passing decision. The holder or authorized administrator executes a grant specifying exact mark/artwork, scope, validity, display conditions, change/reassessment triggers, suspension, revocation, notices and appeal procedure. Preserve the instrument and publish the non-sensitive grant record and status.

## Review and disclosure

Public records contain sufficient identifiers, scope, digests and receipts to verify what was registered. Sensitive identity documents, private contact details and signature credentials remain in the designated private custody process. The holder approves disclosure of its legal name and commitment scope. Changes preserve earlier instruments and record reasons; they do not silently replace a signature, broaden a grant or erase a withdrawal.
