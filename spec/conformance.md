# Conformance and stability gates — draft 03

No stable or complete-framework conformance claim is made by draft 03. The [machine-readable map](conformance.json) records every top-level requirement ID, representative test cases and limitations. It is a coverage index, not a completed independent assessment. Existing detailed [binding criteria](bindings/CONFORMANCE-draft-03.md) remain applicable when a candidate is selected.

An implementation's report MUST identify the source commit, core draft, application, selected bindings, algorithms, role, environment and relying-party policy. It MUST distinguish implemented behavior, local automated evidence, independent interoperability, platform assessment and unresolved requirements. A test file name alone does not establish that every requirement in a profile has been tested.

## Draft research acceptance

The immediate baseline is MTC/PQC for long-term documents: standalone issuance, an authorized signature, a trusted existence bound, retained status and authority inputs, offline verification, and timely preservation renewal across certificate expiry and issuer retirement. TLS deployment, a second complete implementation and an independent security assessment are not current research prerequisites. Those external assurance activities remain stability evidence.

Draft acceptance requires a bounded implementable contract, a runnable lifecycle and positive/negative vectors, including explicit results for missing evidence, late renewal and newly known compromise. The [preservation example](../docs/document-preservation.md) separates historical validation, preserved integrity and current admissibility. Its detached ERS result is distinct from the separate [selected CAdES container profile](../docs/cades-preservation.md).

The [draft acceptance scope](../docs/draft-acceptance.md) records the selected result, original-byte replay, crash-recovery and historical organization-verification boundaries, their executable evidence and persistent limits.

- The SDK reports stable identity and reason fields with separate dimensions for completed checks. Selected CMS, COSE and native mdoc boundaries distinguish malformed input, missing evidence, unimplemented capabilities and failed applicable checks. An established invalid signature or authenticated revocation is not erased by another missing dependency; unsupported plans do not select weaker fallbacks. The named tests bound this classification and do not establish coverage of every algorithm or external container.
- The [document binding](bindings/DOCUMENT-draft-03.md) specifies and exercises direct organizational authority-to-operation authorization, certified KEM recipient admission, encrypted evidence delivery and two-operator encryption-key recovery. The [organization-delegation application](../docs/organization-delegation.md) defines one bounded attenuating path, an exact roster of independent WebAuthn approvals and current CMS execution admission under `CURRENT_STATE_ONLY`. A separate [historical verifier](../docs/organization-history.md) evaluates the retained X509/CMS package at its first complete-package ERS proof time, current historical admissibility and preservation through latest knowledge, without granting current execution permission. Generated real-fixture tests and a separate [saved public-byte corpus](../reference/vectors/organization-history-rar2/README.md) exercise this selection. The [declarative source](../docs/organization-history-source.md) accepts public configuration and exact signed knowledge; an installed-SDK consumer replays the complete expected results offline through its public factories. The optional dedicated SQLite source reauthenticates raw material across process restarts, retains authenticated negative knowledge and returns an explicit commit receipt. Real process-crash and competing-writer tests exercise atomic retention; whole-database rollback without an external anchor remains undetectable. Selected credential CertificateStatus and external OrganizationDelegationStatus may each carry an RFC 4998 proof over the exact complete CMS. Their exact status authority is admitted at publication S and first status proof P_status under knowledge K, with S <= P_status <= K and continuous protection through K. Organization status retains exact typed-target admission, original-package P obligations and original-dependent raw replay; duplicate CMS bytes do not waive an attached proof or P admission. These proofs do not replace current positive coverage or retained negative knowledge. Organizational mdoc, arbitrary graphs, per-object proofs for original organization-package roles retired before the first complete-package proof, independently assessed identity/custody and a second complete implementation remain open.
- The optional single-CMS original CertificateStatus proof derives its exact status bytes, signer, target and scope from the original. Its first status bound and initial TSA must be admitted under historical knowledge; its preservation chain remains protected through current knowledge. Status authority is checked at publication and the earlier admitted status/complete-package bound. Missing supplemental evidence cannot hide independently invalid authority, and the complete-original historical visibility requirement remains. Native mdoc and organization multi-status selections remain unsupported by this option.
- CMS and native mdoc attested plans support required RFC 3161 evidence and conservative status evaluation at the proof-of-existence upper bound. Status refresh preserves the original time proof. TSA clock operations, archival status availability and time composition with raw Passkey/execution-binding plans remain separate gaps.
- Government identity admission and native signer mdoc have separate contracts. A synthetic combined government identity/signing fixture is present; external issuer and independent implementation evaluation remain unrecorded.
- External primitive/parser checks are useful evidence. A second independently developed implementation of the complete CertConcord document baseline has not been recorded.
- The [public original-byte corpus](../reference/vectors/document-acceptance-mtc07-rar2-mdoc2/README.md) supplies explicit external trust inputs, fixed knowledge times and exact expected results for document verification, ERS renewal and retained historical/current decisions. Its offline runner and installed-SDK document replay support CC-DOC-06 draft acceptance. Saved fixtures are synthetic and do not establish operational archive assurance or a second implementation.
- Ordinary WebAuthn completes the selected CMS and mdoc trusted-time plans without raw-signing or EBP evidence. Those proposals are optional and do not gate the document baseline.
- Fixed-root watermarks, scoped authority history, dual-quorum retained root transitions, recovery-target validation and atomic wrapper/archive failure traces are covered locally. Emergency root replacement, distributed operation and production parser evaluation remain unresolved assurance obligations; see the [composition review](../docs/composition-review.md).
- Security/privacy review, published threat-model dispositions, full requirement-to-vector coverage and an external assessment are required before a stability proposal. Existing fuzzing and unit tests do not replace them.
- RFC 4998 preservation and the separate selected RFC 6283 XMLERS evaluator check original protection, TSA rotation and tree-hash lifetimes with explicit external policy. XMLERS authenticates canonical XML renewal inputs and preserves the integrity-only helper boundary; the complete retained-document flows continue to select DER ERS. - The selected CAdES adapter supplies single-signer DER CMS B/T/LT/LTA validation with ATSv3, P-256 signatures, direct-root paths and full direct CRLs. Independent pyHanko digest/path/modification results are distinct from its full AdES LTA procedure, whose CMS signature-timestamp requirement limits the selected document-timestamp-only route.

## Stable specification gate

A stable version can be proposed only after:

1. Every mandatory shared-core and document-baseline requirement has a complete normative contract, machine-readable structures, unambiguous algorithm selection and error behavior.
2. Every mandatory requirement maps to executable positive and negative vectors or an explicit assessed operational control; unmet requirements are resolved, not hidden by a release label.
3. A second independently developed implementation reproduces the mandatory flows and records interoperability and negative-case results.
4. Threat assumptions, privacy/linkability, downgrade protection, compromise/recovery, historical verification and key-custody claims receive documented review; blocking findings are resolved.
5. Required upstream drafts are pinned and their changes assessed; original constructs have a stated analysis and review status. Compatibility and deviations are accurate.
6. Attribution, contribution records, specification scope and actual patent commitments are reported without inventing signatories or grants.
7. Maintainers deliberately approve an immutable candidate and its supported scope. Local test success does not automatically promote a draft or create a release.

The [standalone timestamp application](../docs/timestamp-application.md) has a selected RFC 3161 request, issuance, verification and durable recovery contract. Its P-256/direct-root/full-CRL profile uses explicit current trust and clock admission. Operational time assurance, assessed custody and a second complete independent implementation remain separate obligations.

The [selected S/MIME application](../docs/smime-application.md) protects complete MIME entities with opaque P-256 SignedData and optional RSA-OAEP/AES-GCM encryption under current mailbox, certificate and key admission. Its ordinary OpenSSL exchange checks selected bytes and cryptographic interoperability. Full receiving behavior, transport operation, historical message preservation, durable replay service and a second complete independent implementation remain outside that result. These applications share the same core and have their own conformance boundaries.
